Research

Where the constraints are people, not just parts.

Methodologically: large‑scale deterministic and stochastic optimization, dynamic programming, Markov decision processes, and machine learning including reinforcement learning and LLM applications. Applied to supply chain decision‑making, logistics, inventory management, and energy and smart manufacturing systems.

current — postdoctoral project

Ability‑Aware Stochastic Assembly Line Balancing

A bi‑objective ε‑constraint model that redesigns assembly workflows to balance production efficiency against workforce inclusion under uncertainty — solved via sample average approximation and Benders decomposition. Part of ongoing NSF‑supported work at Michigan State University.

dissertation work

Dynamic Pricing for Perishable Inventory

An exact pricing algorithm for revenue maximization under the logit demand function, and reinforcement‑learning policies for age‑sensitive markdown decisions — presented at INFORMS 2025 and IISE 2026.

infrastructure planning

Alternative‑Fuel Vehicle Station Location

Finite dominating set formulations for refueling station placement, extended to a two‑stage stochastic deployment model for fixed and mobile EV charging under demand uncertainty.

order allocation

Multi‑Item, Multi‑Period Order Allocation

A finite dominating set approach to order allocation under all‑unit quantity discounts and blending ratios, published in the International Journal of Production Economics.

applied OR

Humanitarian & Disaster Logistics

Network design for post‑disaster debris management and robust relief‑distribution and evacuation planning under uncertainty.

methodological base

Robust & Stochastic Optimization

Foundational methods work from my M.Sc. at Pusan National University, underpinning the solution approaches used across the projects above.
